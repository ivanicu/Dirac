# Knowledge Projection Brief

**Status:** normative design; Phase 5 target

## Human question

What governed evidence exists, why should it be trusted, what does it support or
contradict, and what becomes invalid when it changes?

## Entry profiles and cost

Scientists across domains (frequent readers), curator/governance reviewer
(admission/retraction authority), data scientist (large-scale provenance),
external collaborator (restricted).

## Primary projection

Global search, evidence graph, claim/evidence comparison, dataset/source release
inspector, provenance lineage, admission/review queue, and invalidation impact
view. Knowledge is a cross-cutting tool and deep-linkable surface, not a forced
linear stage.

## Actions and scale

Search/query permission-safe aggregates; inspect source/version/method; admit,
review, link, annotate, retract, supersede, preserve snapshot, and notify
affected accountable roles. Large graphs use server-side neighborhood queries,
facets, sampling disclosure, and windowed layouts.

## Failure and security

Search suggestions, counts, timing, graph gaps, and derived-set sizes cannot
leak inaccessible entities. Retraction propagates freshness/invalidation to
derived claims and action previews. External source updates preserve release
identity and retrieval time rather than silently overwriting content.

## Acceptance witness

Search across a large mixed-permission graph without leakage, admit a versioned
release, connect evidence to a hypothesis, retract it, and observe dependent
scores, previews, attention items, and decisions become stale rather than vanish.
