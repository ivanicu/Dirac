05 · Voice

The product never decorates a failure. Canonical strings:

· “SCF did not converge — refusing to ship a decorative field.”
· “Gasteiger cannot parameterize F/P — the QM potential (mep_qm) handles it.”
· “Ligand changed while computing — stale field discarded.”
· “All 5 fields cached in browser + auto-saved to DB.”

Rules: name the actor (backend refused, not "error occurred"); point at the working path; numbers carry their uncertainty; a zero is never reported as a measurement.
